# story-bank

The spine of career-kit. Read `spine/00-principles.md`, then `spine/rubrics/story-standard.md`, then the workflow:

| The user wants to | Follow |
|---|---|
| Build the spine from their material (first time, or big change) | `spine/01-capture/capture.md` |
| Update it (monthly capture, project or role debrief, prune) | `spine/02-maintain/maintain.md` |
| Extract from it (resume lines, STAR answers, promotion evidence) | `spine/03-use/use.md` |

The data lives in `my-files/profile.md` and `my-files/story-bank.md` at the repo root, load them before interviewing, never re-ask what they answer, never commit or transmit them.
