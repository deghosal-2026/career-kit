# Spine examples

One fictional person — Maya R. — through the spine format. Her material is invented; any resemblance to a real person is accidental. Copy the shapes, never the content.

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `maya/profile.md` | a filled profile: statuses, timeline with team context, skills linked to stories, open questions |
| `maya/story-bank.md` | a filled bank: a Strong story with numbers, a Strong story with a named beneficiary and no number, a Weak story with its `[CONFIRM]`s, a quiet month |

To exercise the spine end to end: run `../01-capture/capture.md` on the persona in `../tests/`, then compare against `expected.md` there.
