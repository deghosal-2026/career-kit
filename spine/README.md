# The spine: story bank

Career-kit's evidence core: one profile and one bank of stories that record what you actually did, every fact sourced. Every util reads the spine instead of re-asking you. A resume line, a STAR answer, a promotion case, a cover-letter proof point, all extractions from the same bank.

This directory holds the util. The spine **data** lives in `my-files/` at the kit root (git-ignored) so every util shares it: `my-files/profile.md` and `my-files/story-bank.md`.

## The three workflows

| Workflow | What it does |
|---|---|
| `01-capture/capture.md` | Bootstrap the spine: mine your resume and other material into stories, then fill the highest-value gaps with a short interview |
| `02-maintain/maintain.md` | Keep it current: a monthly capture, project and role-change debriefs, pruning |
| `03-use/use.md` | Draw on it: rules and formats for extracting resume lines, STAR answers, promotion evidence, and proof points |

## Files

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `00-principles.md` | this util's constitution |
| `rubrics/story-standard.md` | the story format, labels, and evidence rules |
| `templates/` | fill-in forms for the profile and each story |
| `examples/` | one fictional person's profile and bank |
| `tests/` | fake personas with expected answer keys |

## How to use

- **In any AI chat:** paste `START.md` and follow what it asks.
- **With an AI agent that reads files:** say "build my story bank", the agent follows `AGENTS.md`.
