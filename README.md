# career-kit

A private, Markdown-only career operating system for people in tech: one evidence spine — your story bank — with sovereign utils around it, covering the whole lifecycle. Works with any LLM and every major harness. No code, no accounts, no telemetry. Your material stays on your machine.

## Start here

- **The charter:** [`WHAT-THIS-IS.md`](WHAT-THIS-IS.md) — what this is, what it is not. Read it first; everything follows it.
- **New here?** [`USER-GUIDE.md`](USER-GUIDE.md) — what you get and how the steps fit together.
- **What's next?** [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md) — shipped in v0.1.0, planned for v0.2.0.
- **Using an AI agent:** open [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).
- **In any AI chat:** open a util's `START.md` and paste its files as it instructs.

## The map

| Area | What it is |
|---|---|
| `spine/` | The story bank: build, maintain, and draw on your profile and sourced stories — the core every util consumes |
| `utils/job-search/` | Resume check, JD match, cover letter, LinkedIn, tracker, interview prep, outreach |
| `utils/growth/` | Promotion packets, self-reviews, ladder planning, public work |
| `utils/offers/` | Offer evaluation, negotiation |
| `utils/transitions/` | Career gaps, discipline switches, exits and returns |
| `shared/` | Cross-util rubrics; graduates here when a second util needs them |
| `my-files/` | Your private material, git-ignored. The spine data lives here |

Built: the spine (v0.1) and the first three job-search utils — resume check, JD match, cover letter (all v0.1, 2026-11-18). Everything else under `utils/` is planned and built when a real need appears — see [`CONVENTIONS.md`](CONVENTIONS.md).

## Privacy

Everything in any `my-files/` directory is ignored by git. Remove your phone number and home address before pasting material into a hosted AI tool.
