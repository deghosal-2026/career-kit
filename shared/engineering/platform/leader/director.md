# Platform / Infra / DevOps engineering Leader: Director

Top of the Leader track in career-kit. VP and above are out of scope.

## In one line
Leads a platform or infrastructure org of several teams and managers, often 25 to 100+ people. Owns platform strategy, org design, and budget, often including infrastructure spend, and partners with executives on how much to invest in the platform.

## Evidence by dimension

| Dimension | Strong | Weak | Absent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Org size and structure stated, plus budget and infrastructure spend where they own them. *"55 engineers across 6 teams and 5 managers; $20M infrastructure budget."* | Several teams, no managers or org shape | One or two teams |
| **Ambiguity** | Set the org's multi-year platform strategy; reorganised the org; decided what to build, buy, or stop | Executes a strategy set by leaders above | No shaping |
| **Depth** | Set the architecture principles and quality bar for how the company builds, ships, and runs software; major platform bets with outcomes | *"Drove engineering excellence"* | None shown |
| **Influence** | Shaped company planning; works with executives and finance; led cross-org initiatives that product engineering adopted | Influence within own org only | None shown |
| **People** | Built a leadership bench; manages managers; succession; engagement and attrition data; org design | Hiring counts only | None |
| **Business impact** | Org outcomes tied to company goals: infrastructure cost as a share of revenue, engineering speed across the company, risk reduced; budget figures | A list of projects delivered | No outcomes |

**Bar for a fit:** all dimensions Strong. Depth may be Weak if Influence and Business impact are very strong.

## Anti-signals
- Delivery bullets that read like a Senior Manager.
- No org size or budget.
- No strategy or org design.
- No evidence of developing leaders.
- Impact described as platform features shipped, not as what changed for the company.
- Cost cuts with no link to what the business gained or kept.
- Startup "Head of" title with Manager scope.

## Weak to Strong

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Led infrastructure engineering. | Led platform and infrastructure (55 engineers, 6 teams, 5 managers, $20M infrastructure budget); set the 2-year strategy that moved every product team onto one self-service platform. |
| Reduced cloud spend. | Partnered with the CFO to make each product line own its infrastructure spend; cost per customer fell 35% over 2 years while revenue grew 60%. |
| Improved developer experience. | Reorganised 4 tool-based teams into 6 teams aligned to developer journeys; new services reached production in 3 days instead of 4 weeks, and developer satisfaction rose 22 points. |

## What holds people back at Director
- Impact not tied to company goals.
- No org size, or no budget figures when they do own a budget. Not every company gives Directors a budget.
- Reads like a Senior Manager with a bigger team.
- No evidence of strategy or org design decisions.
- The platform is described as a cost centre, with no case for what it is worth to the company.
