# Platform / Infra / DevOps engineering Leader: Senior Manager

## In one line
Leads several platform or infrastructure teams, often through managers or tech leads, roughly 15 to 40 people. Sets charters, plans headcount, runs the platform like a product for internal teams, and delivers across teams.

## Evidence by dimension

| Dimension | Strong | Weak | Absent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Several teams with structure and users stated. *"Led 3 teams (24 engineers, 2 managers) owning build, deploy, and environments for 600 developers."* | One large team, no structure | One team |
| **Ambiguity** | Defined team charters; reorganised teams around user journeys; turned engineering goals into a multi-team platform roadmap | Managed teams with charters already set | No shaping |
| **Depth** | Arbitrated cross-team technical trade-offs; set platform architecture direction with Staff engineers | Team-level technical calls | No technical credibility shown |
| **Influence** | Shaped org planning; partnered with product engineering leaders and finance on platform investment and cost; changed priorities across the org | Influence within own teams only | None shown |
| **People** | Grew managers (first-time managers developed); built the hiring pipeline; planned succession | Hired ICs only | None |
| **Business impact** | Outcomes across teams tied to engineering goals: adoption across the org, delivery speed, cloud cost; owned a slice of headcount or budget | Team-level outcomes | No outcomes |

**Bar for a fit:** Scope, People, and Business impact Strong. Ambiguity ideally Strong. Depth and Influence at least Weak.

## Anti-signals
- Reads like a one-team manager scaled up.
- No managers or tech leads reporting to them.
- No planning, headcount, or hiring plans.
- Platform success measured by features shipped, not by adoption or user outcomes.
- Outcomes listed as projects, not results.

## Weak to Strong

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Managed the platform teams. | Led 3 teams (24 engineers, 2 managers) owning build, deploy, and environments for 600 developers; set charters around the path from first commit to production. |
| Hired a lot of engineers. | Built the hiring plan for 10 roles in a year and filled 9; developed 2 tech leads into first-time managers. |
| Delivered the new developer platform. | Delivered the self-service platform across 3 teams; 85% of services moved onto it within 18 months, and median lead time for changes fell from 5 days to 1. |

## What separates Senior Manager from Director
A Director resume shows:
- An org of several teams and managers, often 25 to 100+ people.
- The org's strategy and structure, not only its delivery.
- Budget ownership, including infrastructure spend where the company gives Directors a budget.
- A leadership bench: managers of managers, succession.
- Partnership with executives on platform investment and infrastructure cost.
- Outcomes tied to company goals.
