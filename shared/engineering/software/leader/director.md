# Software engineering Leader: Director

Top of the Leader track in career-kit. VP and above are out of scope.

## In one line
Leads an org of several teams and managers, often 25 to 100+ people. Owns strategy, org design, and budget, and partners with executives.

## Evidence by dimension

| Dimension | Strong | Weak | Absent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Org size and structure stated, plus budget where they own one. *"60 engineers across 7 teams and 5 managers."* | Several teams, no managers or org shape | One or two teams |
| **Ambiguity** | Set the org's multi-year strategy; reorganised the org; took it into a new area; stopped initiatives that weren't creating value | Executes a strategy set by leaders above | No shaping |
| **Depth** | Set the engineering quality bar or architecture principles for the org; major technical bets with outcomes | *"Drove technical excellence"* | None shown |
| **Influence** | Shaped company planning; works with executives; led cross-org initiatives | Influence within own org only | None shown |
| **People** | Built a leadership bench; manages managers and grew managers who now manage managers; set the org's hiring bar; succession; engagement and attrition data; org design, including where senior technical leaders sit | Hiring counts only | None |
| **Business impact** | Org outcomes tied to company goals; budget figures; contribution to revenue or cost | A list of projects delivered | No outcomes |

**Bar for a fit:** all dimensions Strong. Depth may be Weak if Influence and Business impact are very strong.

## Anti-signals
- Delivery bullets that read like a Senior Manager.
- No org size, or no budget figures when they own a budget.
- No strategy or org design.
- No evidence of developing leaders.
- Impact described as features shipped.
- Startup "Director" with Manager scope.

## Weak to Strong

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Led payments engineering. | Led payments engineering (60 engineers, 7 teams, 5 managers, $12M budget); set the 2-year strategy that moved the company onto its own payment processing. |
| Improved engineering culture. | Redesigned the org from 4 component teams into 7 product-aligned teams; lead time for changes fell from 9 days to 2 and engagement scores rose 14 points. |
| Worked with executives. | Partnered with the CFO and product leadership on the processor migration, cutting processing fees by $3.1M a year. |

## What holds people back at Director
- Impact not tied to company goals.
- No org size, or no budget figures when they do own a budget. Not every company gives Directors a budget.
- Reads like a Senior Manager with a bigger team.
- No evidence of strategy or org design decisions.
