# Software engineering Leader: Senior Manager

## In one line
Leads several teams, often through managers or tech leads, roughly 15 to 40 people. Sets charters, plans headcount, and delivers across teams.

## Evidence by dimension

| Dimension | Strong | Weak | Absent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Several teams with structure stated. *"Led 3 teams (22 engineers, 2 managers) owning search and discovery."* | One large team, no structure | One team of usual size, nothing beyond it |
| **Ambiguity** | Defined team charters; reorganised teams around goals; turned business strategy into a multi-team roadmap over 6 to 12 months; stopped or cut projects that weren't paying off | Managed teams with charters already set | No shaping |
| **Depth** | Arbitrated cross-team technical trade-offs; set architecture direction with Staff engineers | Team-level technical calls | No technical credibility shown |
| **Influence** | Shaped org planning; partnered with peer leaders and product leadership; changed priorities across the org | Influence within own teams only | None shown |
| **People** | Grew managers (first-time managers developed) and holds them accountable; grew senior ICs into tech lead roles; built the hiring pipeline and longer-term headcount plan; planned succession | Hired ICs only | None |
| **Business impact** | Outcomes across teams tied to business goals; owned a slice of headcount or budget | Team-level outcomes | No outcomes |

**Bar for a fit:** Scope, People, and Business impact Strong. Ambiguity ideally Strong. Depth and Influence at least Weak.

## Anti-signals
- Reads like a one-team manager scaled up.
- No managers or tech leads reporting to them.
- No planning, headcount, or hiring plans.
- Outcomes listed as projects, not results.

## Weak to Strong

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Managed multiple teams. | Led 3 teams (22 engineers, 2 managers) across search and recommendations; set team charters and a shared roadmap tied to revenue goals. |
| Hired a lot of engineers. | Built the hiring plan for 12 roles in a year and filled 11; developed 2 tech leads into first-time managers. |
| Delivered the new search platform. | Delivered the search platform rebuild across 3 teams on schedule; search conversion rose 9% and infrastructure cost per query fell 30%. |

## What separates Senior Manager from Director
A Director resume shows:
- An org of several teams and managers, often 25 to 100+ people.
- The org's strategy and structure, not only its delivery.
- Budget ownership, where the company gives Directors a budget.
- A leadership bench: managers of managers, succession.
- Partnership with executives.
- Outcomes tied to company goals.
