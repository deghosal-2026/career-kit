# Data engineering Leader: Director

Top of the Leader track in career-kit. VP and above are out of scope.

## In one line
Leads an org of several data teams and managers, often 25 to 100+ people. Owns data strategy, org design, and budget, and partners with executives on how the company uses, governs, and pays for data.

If the role also covers analytics or data science, use this file for the leadership evidence and judge craft Depth on the data engineering and platform side. Those other functions are out of scope in career-kit.

## Evidence by dimension

| Dimension | Strong | Weak | Absent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Org size and structure stated, plus budget where they own one. *"55 engineers across 6 teams and 4 managers, $9M including data compute."* | Several teams, no managers or org shape | One or two teams |
| **Ambiguity** | Set the org's multi-year data strategy; chose the operating model (central, embedded, or domain-owned data); took the org into a new area such as real-time data | Executes a strategy set by leaders above | No shaping |
| **Depth** | Set the quality bar, data standards, and architecture principles for the org; major platform bets with outcomes | *"Drove data excellence"* | None shown |
| **Influence** | Shaped company planning; works with executives on data, cost, and risk; led cross-org initiatives such as company-wide metric definitions or privacy programs | Influence within own org only | None shown |
| **People** | Built a leadership bench; manages managers; succession; engagement and attrition data; org design | Hiring counts only | None |
| **Business impact** | Org outcomes tied to company goals; budget figures; contribution to revenue, cost, or regulatory risk; data trusted for company decisions | A list of platforms and migrations delivered | No outcomes |

**Bar for a fit:** all dimensions Strong. Depth may be Weak if Influence and Business impact are very strong.

## Anti-signals
- Delivery bullets that read like a Senior Manager.
- No org size or budget.
- No data strategy or org design.
- No evidence of developing leaders.
- Impact described as platforms built or tables delivered.
- Startup "Head of Data" with Manager scope.

## Weak to Strong

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Led data engineering. | Led data engineering (55 engineers, 6 teams, 4 managers, $9M budget including compute); set the 2-year strategy that moved the company from a central request queue to domain-owned data on a shared platform. |
| Improved trust in company data. | Ran the program to define 40 company metrics once, with owners and tests; the board pack moved from 2 weeks of manual reconciliation to automated delivery on day 3 of each month. |
| Worked with executives on costs. | Partnered with the CFO on data spend; renegotiated contracts and retired 3 overlapping systems, cutting $2.1M a year while supporting 2x data growth. |

## What holds people back at Director
- Impact not tied to company goals.
- No org size, or no budget figures when they do own a budget. Not every company gives Directors a budget.
- Reads like a Senior Manager with a bigger team.
- No evidence of data strategy or org design decisions.
- Mixed data roles described so broadly that the data engineering leadership cannot be seen.
