# PM-T Leader: Director

Top of the Leader track in career-kit. VP and above are out of scope.

PM orgs are smaller than engineering orgs. The 25 to 100+ range in `levels.md` is an engineering figure. A product Director of 20 PMs may shape the work of 150 or more engineers; credit both, but judge People on the product org they manage.

## In one line
Leads a product org of several groups and PM managers, often 10 to 30 or more PMs. Owns product strategy for a business line, org design, and often a budget or P&L, and partners with executives.

## Evidence by dimension

| Dimension | Strong | Weak | Absent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Org size, structure, and business line stated, plus budget or P&L where they own one. *"18 PMs across 4 groups and 4 Group PMs, owning the $90M developer platform business."* | Several PM groups, no managers or org shape | One or two PM teams No PM org at all is Absent |
| **Ambiguity** | Set the multi-year product strategy for a business line; reorganised the product org; took it into a new market or segment | Executes a strategy set by leaders above | No shaping |
| **Depth** | Set the product bar for the org: how strategy, discovery, and metrics are done; made major bets on markets, platforms, or pricing, with outcomes | *"Drove product excellence"* | None shown |
| **Influence** | Shaped company planning and investment; works with executives; led cross-org initiatives with engineering, sales, and finance | Influence within own org only | None shown |
| **People** | Built a bench of PM leaders; manages Group PMs; succession; engagement and attrition data; designed the product org | Hiring counts only | None |
| **Business impact** | Business-line outcomes tied to company goals: revenue, growth, retention, margin; budget or P&L figures | A list of launches | No outcomes |

**Bar for a fit:** all dimensions Strong. Depth may be Weak if Influence and Business impact are very strong.

## Anti-signals
- Roadmap and launch bullets that read like a Senior Manager.
- No org size, business line, or financial figures.
- No strategy or org design.
- No evidence of developing PM leaders.
- Impact described as features shipped.
- Startup "Director" or "Head of Product" with one or two PMs reporting.

## Weak to Strong

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Led product for the developer platform. | Led the developer platform product org (18 PMs, 4 Group PMs) for a $90M business line; set the 2-year strategy to sell to enterprises, growing revenue from $62M to $90M. |
| Restructured the product team. | Reorganised 4 groups split by technical layer into 4 groups aligned to customer journeys; time from customer request to roadmap decision fell from 10 weeks to 3, and PM attrition dropped from 22% to 9%. |
| Worked with executives on strategy. | Partnered with the CFO and sales leadership on the decision to partner rather than build a hosted offering; avoided an estimated $8M build and reached 140 joint customers in the first year. |

## What holds people back at Director
- Impact not tied to company goals or to a business line's results.
- No org size, or no budget or P&L figures when they do own one. Not every company gives product Directors a budget.
- Reads like a Senior Manager with a bigger team.
- No evidence of strategy or org design decisions.
- Technical depth on show, but no market, pricing, or investment calls.
