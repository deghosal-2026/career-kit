# TPM Leader: Senior Manager

## In one line
Leads a TPM group of roughly 8 to 20 people, often through 1 to 3 TPM managers, covering the program portfolio of an org. Sets where TPMs work, plans headcount, and delivers across the portfolio.

Team size here is smaller than the engineering Senior Manager size in `levels.md`. TPM groups are small relative to the engineering orgs they serve; also credit the size of the engineering org the portfolio covers.

## Evidence by dimension

| Dimension | Strong | Weak | Absent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Group size and structure stated, plus coverage. *"Led 14 TPMs (2 managers) covering the 30-team platform org."* | One larger team, no structure | One small team |
| **Ambiguity** | Defined TPM charters for several areas; decided which programs get TPM support; turned org strategy into a portfolio plan | Ran a portfolio someone else set | No shaping |
| **Depth** | Arbitrated trade-offs between programs competing for the same teams; set standards for program plans, risk, and launch readiness **across several areas or the org** | Program-level judgment only | No technical or program credibility shown |
| **Influence** | Shaped org planning; partnered with engineering and product directors; changed priorities across the org | Influence within own group only | None shown |
| **People** | Grew TPM managers (first-time managers developed); built the TPM hiring pipeline; planned succession | Hired TPMs only | None |
| **Business impact** | Portfolio outcomes tied to business goals: planning accuracy, major launches landed, time to market, risk removed; owned TPM headcount | Team-level outcomes | No outcomes |

**Bar for a fit:** Scope, People, and Business impact Strong. Ambiguity ideally Strong. Depth and Influence at least Weak.

## Anti-signals
- Reads like a one-team TPM manager scaled up.
- No managers or Staff TPMs reporting to them.
- No planning, headcount, or coverage decisions.
- Outcomes listed as programs, not results.

## Weak to Strong

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Managed multiple TPM teams. | Led 14 TPMs (2 managers) covering the 30-team platform org; set TPM charters by area and ended TPM support for 4 low-risk programs to staff the 2 riskiest. |
| Built the TPM team. | Built the hiring plan for 7 TPM roles in a year and filled 6; developed 2 Senior TPMs into first-time managers. |
| Delivered the platform roadmap. | Ran the platform org's portfolio of 11 programs; introduced a single quarterly plan across 30 teams, and the share of committed work delivered rose from 60% to 82% in a year. |

## What separates Senior Manager from Director
A Director resume shows:
- A TPM org of several managers, often 15 to 50+ people, or the whole TPM function at a smaller company.
- The operating model for planning and delivery across the org or company, not only the portfolio.
- Budget or headcount ownership, where the company gives Directors one.
- A leadership bench: managers of managers, succession.
- Partnership with executives on company planning.
- Outcomes tied to company goals.
