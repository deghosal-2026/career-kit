# TPM IC: Staff

## In one line
Runs the programs that matter most to an org: several related programs, a portfolio, or one high-risk program across orgs. Finds and frames cross-team problems nobody assigned, and sets how the org plans and delivers.

## Shapes of Staff
Staff TPM work comes in several common shapes. All are valid. Judge each by its own evidence; do not mark someone down for not being a different shape.

| Shape | What it looks like | Where Scope comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Program lead** | Runs one very large, high-risk program across orgs | The program's size, risk, and importance to the business |
| **Portfolio owner** | Owns a set of related programs for an org and the trade-offs between them | The number of programs and teams in the portfolio |
| **Operating model builder** | Designs how an org plans, launches, and handles cross-team risk | How many teams adopt the process and what improved |
| **Right hand** | Extends a senior engineering leader's reach: planning, priorities, cross-org escalations | The leader's org |

## Evidence by dimension

| Dimension | Strong | Weak | Absent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Several programs or one program across orgs. *"Ran the region expansion program: 14 teams in 3 orgs, 9 months."* | One large program inside one org | One team's projects |
| **Ambiguity** | Spotted an unassigned cross-team problem, built the case, and got it staffed or prioritised | Ran a large program someone else defined | Executes assigned programs |
| **Depth** | Pushed architecture-level trade-offs to a decision across teams; wrote decision documents leaders used; can go deep on any system in the program when a risk appears | Deep in one team's systems only | No technical content beyond dates and status |
| **Influence** | Aligned directors and teams with conflicting goals; a planning, launch, or risk process they designed became the org standard | *"Partnered with leadership"* | Only own program teams. A blocker for Staff |
| **People** | Grew Senior TPMs (promotions); raised the TPM hiring bar; coached engineering leads on running cross-team work | Mentors newer TPMs only | None |
| **Business impact** | Org-level outcomes: time to market across a product line, a major launch landed, risk removed, engineering time or cost saved across teams | One program's dates | No outcomes |

**Bar for a fit:** Scope, Ambiguity, and Influence Strong. For the Program lead shape, Scope can be Strong through the risk and importance of one program even when it is the only one. Depth and Business impact Strong, or Weak with a clear story. People at least Weak.

## Anti-signals
- Every bullet sits inside one org's program.
- Cross-org work described with *supported, helped, facilitated*.
- No written direction: no decision documents, program strategy, or standards.
- Outcomes measured only as dates hit.
- A long list of programs with no sense of which mattered most.

## Weak to Strong

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Managed programs for the infrastructure org. | Owned the portfolio of 5 infrastructure programs across 9 teams; moved 2 teams off a lower-value program to unblock the storage migration, which finished a quarter early and saved $900K a year. |
| Improved the planning process. | Found that a third of each quarter's committed work slipped on hidden cross-team dependencies; designed a dependency review used by 11 teams, and slips fell from 32% to 12% in 3 quarters. |
| Ran the region expansion. | Proposed and ran the expansion into a second region across 14 teams in 3 orgs; wrote the readiness criteria, got directors to commit dates, and launched in 9 months with no customer-facing incidents. |

## What separates Staff from Principal
A Principal resume shows:
- Programs that span the company, not one org.
- Multi-year direction: turning company strategy into a set of programs.
- Influence on leadership decisions: what to fund, what to stop, how to sequence big bets.
- Programs that reach beyond engineering: legal, finance, operations, partners.
- Growing Staff TPMs and shaping the TPM function.
- Outcomes that change the company's speed, cost base, or risk.
